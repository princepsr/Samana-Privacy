Privacy Policy

Samana · Effective 25 November 2026

The short version
  • Your check-ins, meals, cycle, body details and progress are stored on your phone first. You can use Samana without an account.
  • We never sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • If you sign in, we back up your data so you can restore it on a new phone. Only you can read it.
  • When you ask for a smart meal check, the meal description is sent to our server and an AI service to write the tip.
  • You can delete everything, including cloud copies, from Settings at any time.

Samana is a lifestyle companion for people with PCOS and hormonal health concerns. This policy explains what information the Samana app collects, why, who handles it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Samana mobile app. In this policy, "we", "us" and "Samana" mean Prince Singh (Samana), Bengaluru, India.

Not medical advice

Samana is an informational wellness and symptom tracker. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult your gynaecologist, endocrinologist or another qualified professional about your health.

What we collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier.

What stays on your phone

Your data is written to your phone first, so Samana works offline and without an account. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Voice notes for meals use your phone's own speech recognition on the device, and we do not receive the recording.

What leaves your phone, and when

  • If you sign in, your data is backed up to our database so you can restore it on a new phone. It is protected so that only your account can read it.
  • When you use a smart meal check, the meal description is sent to our server, which sends it to an AI service (Google's Gemini) to write the tip. To keep costs low, common meals and their results are saved in a shared cache. The cache holds the meal text and the result only, not your name or account. Please do not put personal details in a meal note.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider. We never see your card details.
  • Sign-in uses Google or Apple. They tell us your email and an account id, and nothing else we ask for.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your data to decide anything that affects you legally or financially.

How long we keep it

On your phone, until you delete it or uninstall the app. In the cloud, for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete your account and the cloud copies of your data. Shared cache entries do not identify you and may remain. Anonymous usage events are kept for up to 24 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: in the app, open Settings, then "Delete data on this device", or "Delete my account and cloud data" if you are signed in. Deletion cannot be undone.
  • Access, correct or export your data: you can see and edit your logs in the app, and create a doctor summary PDF. For a copy of cloud data, contact us.
  • Reminders: turn them on or off in Settings, or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, India, California, Washington and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any right, write to us at the address below. We will reply within the time the law gives us.

Because your health information is sensitive, we handle it only with your consent, which you give by choosing to enter it and, for cloud backup, by signing in. You can withdraw that consent by deleting your data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and cloud data is protected so each account can read only its own rows. No system is perfectly secure, so please also protect your phone with a screen lock.

Children

Samana is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
