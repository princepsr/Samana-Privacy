Samana, a calm companion for PCOS

Samana is a free app for people living with PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome). It gives you a plan that fits your body, one small step each day, and a cycle tracker that works for irregular cycles. No calorie counting, no guilt.

What Samana does

  • A plan that fits youAnswer a few questions about your symptoms, body and kitchen, and see your pattern and a first-week plan before you pay for anything.
  • Gentle daily stepsOne main thing each day, with an easier version for foggy days, and friendly reminders from a companion you name yourself.
  • Movement that adaptsShort guided sessions with animated figures, matched to your body, energy and symptoms.
  • Food in portionsPlates and palms, not calories, in the foods you actually eat. A smart meal check shows how steady a meal will keep your energy.
  • A cycle tracker for any lengthCycles from 21 to 180 days or more, with no "late" alerts.
  • Insights from your own check-insSee how your sleep, energy and stress move over time, and share a summary with your doctor.

Your data and Google sign-in

Your check-ins, meals, cycle and body details are stored on your phone first, and you can use Samana without an account. If you choose Continue with Google, Samana receives only your email address, name and profile picture, and uses them only to create your account, back up your data and restore it on a new phone. We never sell your data and we do not show ads.
