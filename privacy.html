Privacy Policy

The short version
  • Your check-ins, meals, cycle, body details and progress are stored on your phone first. You can use Samana without an account.
  • If you sign in with Google, we back your data up to our cloud database so you can restore it on a new phone. Only your account can read it.
  • If you ask for a smart meal check, the text of that meal is sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI to write the tip.
  • We never sell your data, we show no ads, and we do not use or share your health data for advertising.
  • You can delete everything, including the cloud copy, from Settings inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Samana is a lifestyle companion app for people with PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome) and related hormonal health concerns. It is made and operated by Prince Singh (Samana), Bengaluru, India, who is the controller of the information described here. In this policy "we", "us" and "Samana" mean that person.

This policy covers the Samana Android app (package com.samana.pcos), our backup and meal-check servers, and the website at samana.sudoexplain.dev. It does not cover other companies' services that you may use alongside Samana, such as Google Play or Google sign-in, which have their own policies.

2. Not medical advice

Samana is an informational wellness and symptom tracker. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult your gynaecologist, endocrinologist or another qualified professional about your health.

Samana does not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, calendar, photos, files, call or SMS history, or your advertising identifier. It has no advertising or tracking SDKs and does not build advertising profiles.

We never receive it. It leaves your phone only if you choose to share it, and then goes wherever you send it.

5. How we use your information

  • To run the app you asked for: to build your plan, choose your day's steps and movement, show your cycle, progress and insights, send reminders you turned on, and give meal tips.
  • To back up and restore: if you sign in, to keep your data safe and bring it to a new phone.
  • To provide Pro: to unlock paid features and restore purchases.
  • To keep the service safe and affordable: to stop abuse of the meal check and to fix problems.
  • To improve Samana: to understand, using event names only, where people get stuck, never using your health data.
  • To meet legal duties and to answer your requests.

We do not use your information to make decisions that affect you legally or financially, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. What stays on your phone and what leaves it

Everyday useYour data is written to storage on your phone first, so Samana works offline and without an account. Please do not put personal details in a meal note. If you have used your free smart checks, or you are offline, a simple check on your phone is used instead and nothing is sent.
Voice inputWhen you tap Speak, Samana uses the speech recognition service built into your Android phone and receives only the resulting text. On Android that service is normally provided by Google, and depending on your phone and settings the audio may be processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy. Samana never receives, stores or uploads the audio.
Sign-inHandled by Google. See section 7.
PurchasesHandled by Google Play and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase id and your purchase status so it can tell Samana whether Pro is active. It does not receive your health data.
Usage eventsEvent names (section 3) are sent to our database using your anonymous or account id.
Reminders and timersScheduled and shown entirely on your phone.

7. Google user data (Sign in with Google)

If you choose "Continue with Google", Samana asks Google for only three basic things: your email address, your name and your profile picture (the standard openid, email and profile permissions). We do not ask for your contacts, calendar, Drive files, location or anything else from your Google account.

  • How we use it: only to create your Samana account, to back up your data, and to restore it when you sign in on a new phone. We use it for nothing else.
  • Sharing: we do not sell Google user data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone except the service provider that hosts the account (Supabase), which processes it only to run Samana.
  • Limited Use: Samana's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No humans read your Google user data, except where needed for security or to comply with the law.
  • No AI training: we do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • Deletion and control: delete your account in the app (Settings, then "Delete my account and cloud data") and your Google-derived account details are deleted with it. You can also remove Samana's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Health information and your consent

Much of what you enter in Samana, such as symptoms, cycle dates, body details and mood, is sensitive health information. We handle it only with your consent, which you give by choosing to enter it and, for cloud backup, by choosing to sign in. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data (section 12). It is optional to enter any of it: you can use many parts of Samana with less information, with a less personalised plan as the only difference. We never use your health information for advertising and we never share it with advertisers, insurers, employers or data brokers.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: in the app, open Settings, then "Delete data on this device", or "Delete my account and cloud data" if you are signed in. Deletion cannot be undone. You can also delete a single check-in or meal under Insights, then "Past days and meals". Steps are on our account deletion page. If you cannot open the app, email us and we will delete your account within 30 days.
  • Access, correct or export: you can see and edit your logs and body details in the app, and create a doctor summary PDF. For a copy of your cloud data, email us.
  • Reminders and permissions: turn reminders on or off in Settings, or manage permissions in your phone's settings.
  • Sign out or stop backup: signing out stops the backup. Your phone keeps working on its own.
  • Your legal rights: depending on where you live (for example India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; the EU and UK under the GDPR; California and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, to withdraw consent, to object, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not discriminate against you for using these rights. To use any right, write to the address in section 17. We reply within the time the law gives us, and in any case within 30 days.

13. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). In the cloud, access is limited by row-level security so each account can read only its own rows, and server keys are kept on the server, never in the app. On your phone, data lives in the app's private storage, and Android's cloud backup of that storage is turned off. No system is perfectly secure, so please also protect your phone with a screen lock. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

14. Storage and transfers abroad

Our providers run servers in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including outside India, the EU and the UK. Where the law requires it, we rely on the providers' standard contractual protections.

15. Children

Samana is for adults aged 18 and over and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the dates at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect. Older versions are available on request.
