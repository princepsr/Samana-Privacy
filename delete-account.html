Delete your Samana account and data

Samana by Prince Singh · Bengaluru, India

Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Samana, tap your profile (the round icon at the top of the Today screen), then Privacy and settings.
  2. Under Delete, tap Delete my account and cloud data if you signed in, or Delete data on this device if you did not.
  3. Confirm. This cannot be undone.

Can't open the app? Ask us to delete it

Email singharnav7177@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Samana account". We will delete your account and cloud data and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record.
  • Your profile, body details, daily logs, symptoms, cycle and period dates, supplements, meals and activity history stored in the cloud.
  • Your companion preferences.
  • Data on your phone is removed when you choose "Delete data on this device" or uninstall the app.

What may remain

  • Anonymous usage events (event names only, with no health data and no name) and shared meal-cache entries that do not identify you.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play or the Apple App Store and RevenueCat, as required for accounting. Cancel any subscription in your store's subscription settings, because deleting your Samana account does not cancel it.
