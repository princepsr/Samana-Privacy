Your free week, Basics and Pro

  • Free week. When you start, you can use everything in Samana for 7 days. You do not need a card or an account. It is a trial inside the app, not a Google Play trial, and nothing is charged.
  • Basics. After the free week you can keep using the free part of Samana (check-ins, mood, cycle calendar, symptom log, your past days, the breathing timer and a helpline) for as long as you like. It has no ads. Everything you logged stays available to you.
  • Pro. Pro is a paid subscription that unlocks the rest of the app and automatic cloud backup while you are signed in. The price in your own currency, and whether it renews monthly or yearly, is shown on the screen before you buy.
